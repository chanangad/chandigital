QRganiser

Put your cards on your Garmin watch

About the app

1Scan your cards

Point your camera at a loyalty card, gym pass or ticket.
QR codes and barcodes both work.

2Check names

Scanned cards appear here. Tap a name to rename it.

3Send to your watch

On your watch open QRganiser, scroll to Add from phone and type the code it shows.

✓
Sent

Your watch picks the cards up within a few seconds.
Keep the Garmin Connect app running on this phone.

Cards are encrypted on this phone with the code your watch shows. Our server only passes the encrypted parcel along and deletes it after 10 minutes. Nothing is stored in this browser.

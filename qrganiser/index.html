Features

Built to be faster than digging out your phone

Scan to add Open the add page on your phone, point the camera at a card and type the short code your watch shows. The card arrives within seconds.
QR codes and real barcodes QR, Code 128 and EAN-13 / UPC-A, which covers most supermarket and loyalty cards. Checkout laser scanners can't read QR codes but can read barcodes.
Boarding passes Scan the barcode on your boarding pass - paper, PDF or airline app. It lands on your watch named after your flight, with the date and seat, moves to the top the day before you fly and removes itself after. Keep your airline's pass as a backup.
A wallet, not a list Your cards sit in a colourful stack. Scroll through them, pin favourites to the top, pick one of eight colours for each, and sort by recently used or A-Z.
Up to 50 cards 30 typed into the app settings in Garmin Connect, each with a name, the code content and a format, plus 20 scanned from your phone.
Built for the scanner Codes are drawn black on white with a quiet zone and whole-pixel modules, and the screen stays lit at full brightness while a code is up.
Screen-safe on AMOLED The light is briefly released once a minute, and the code closes itself after 1, 2 or 5 minutes, so a forgotten card never burns into the screen.
A glance that knows your habits The glance shows how many cards you have and which one you used last, so the card you reach for most is always easy to find.
Quick on the buttons UP and DOWN move through your cards and START shows the code. Hold MENU on a card to pin it, change its colour or format, or read out the number.

Privacy

Your codes stay yours

Every code is generated on the watch, so showing a card needs no phone, no signal and no server. It works in airplane mode.

When you scan a card on your phone, it is encrypted in the browser with a key derived from the code your watch shows, and only your watch can work that key out. Our server passes the encrypted parcel along without being able to read it, and deletes it once the watch collects it or after 10 minutes, whichever comes first. There are no accounts.

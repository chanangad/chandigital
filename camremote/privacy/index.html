Privacy Policy

How Chan Digital handles data for the CamRemote app and website.

Last updated: October 7, 2026

In short: your photos and videos are saved on your phone and never leave it. There are no accounts and no analytics. The Android app has no internet permission at all. The watch sends only its claim code to our server, to check whether the app is unlocked. If you buy the unlock, we keep your name, email and claim code to deliver and support it.

1. Who we are

Chan Digital ("we", "us", "our") makes CamRemote: a Garmin watch app and its phone companion apps that let your watch take photos and videos on your phone. Contact us at .

2. Data kept on your watch

CamRemote stores these on the watch, in Garmin's app storage. None of it is personal data:

  • License state - a claim code derived from the watch, whether the app is unlocked, when the free trial started and when the license was last checked.
  • Review prompt - how many photos or videos you have taken (up to three) and whether you have already been asked for a rating, so you are asked at most once.

The photo count shown on the watch comes from the phone and is not stored.

3. What the watch sends

  • To the CamRemote phone app - "take a photo", "start or stop video" and "are you there?", through Garmin Connect on your phone. The phone answers with the photo count, whether it is recording, and short status messages. This passes through Garmin's apps under Garmin's privacy policy.
  • To our server - only its claim code, to check whether the app is unlocked. This happens when you open the app (at most once a minute while it is not unlocked, weekly once it is) and while the unlock screen is open. Our server notes each code it sees, with the first and last time it checked in and how many times, so the checkout page can tell a real code from a mistyped one and so we can see how many trials begin.
  • Rating - if you choose Rate app, the request goes to Garmin's Connect IQ Store.

4. The Android phone app

  • No internet access - the app does not request Android's internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your phone. It talks only to Garmin Connect on the same phone.
  • Photos and videos - in "Shoot with Camera" mode they are taken and saved by your phone's own camera app. In built-in camera mode CamRemote saves them to Pictures/CamRemote and Movies/CamRemote on your phone. They are never uploaded by CamRemote.
  • Camera and microphone - requested only if you use the built-in camera, and used only to take the photos and videos you ask for.
  • Accessibility service (optional) - in "Shoot with Camera" mode CamRemote uses Android's accessibility service to press your camera app's shutter and record buttons when your watch asks. It is limited to your default camera app, reads only that app's on-screen controls to find those buttons, and only when a command arrives from your watch. It does not read other apps, see your photos, or collect, store or share any data. You turn it on yourself in Settings > Accessibility, after an in-app explanation, and can turn it off at any time.
  • Confirming a photo was saved - CamRemote listens for Android's notice that a new item was added to the media library, so the watch can confirm the photo. The notice contains only an item reference, not the photo, and CamRemote does not open, store or send it. No photo permission is involved.

5. The iPhone app

The iPhone app works the same way with its own camera: it asks for camera and microphone access to take photos and videos, permission to add them to your Photos library, and Bluetooth access, which Garmin's software uses to reach your watch. Nothing is uploaded.

6. Data collected when you buy

When you buy the one-time unlock at chandigital.in/camremote, we collect:

  • Name and email address - to send your receipt and invoice and to help with support or refunds.
  • Claim code - the code your watch shows, which the license is attached to.
  • State (India only) - your state for the GST invoice.
  • Payment details - handled entirely by Razorpay (India), PayPal or Dodo Payments (international). We never see or store card numbers.

7. How we use your data

  • To unlock the app on your watch and keep it unlocked.
  • To send your receipt and invoice by email.
  • To answer support requests and process refunds.

We do not sell, rent or share your personal data for marketing.

8. Where data is stored

License, payment and claim-code records are stored on Cloudflare (Workers and D1), in a database used only for CamRemote. Invoice emails are sent through our email provider. Payment processors keep their own records under their own privacy policies.

9. Cookies and analytics

Neither the apps nor chandigital.in use analytics, advertising or tracking. No tracking cookies are set.

10. Data retention

Purchase records (name, email, claim code) are kept for up to 3 years for support, refunds and legal compliance. Claim-code check-in records contain no personal data and are kept while CamRemote is offered. You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data. Email with "Privacy Request" in the subject line.

12. Children

CamRemote is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version. Continued use of the apps or website after a change means you accept the updated policy.

How it works

Your watch presses the shutter

  1. Install CamRemote on your watch and phone The watch app from Connect IQ, the free phone app on your phone, and Garmin Connect to link them.
  2. Prop up your phone On Android, CamRemote can drive your phone's own camera app, so you keep its full photo quality. Or use CamRemote's built-in camera.
  3. Press START on your watch The photo is taken and your watch buzzes once it is saved. Press UP to start and stop video, with the recording time on your wrist.

Features

Everyone in the photo, including you

Your phone's own camera On Android, CamRemote presses the shutter in your phone's camera app, so photos get all of its processing - HDR, night mode and the rest. It opens the camera app for you if it isn't already open.
Photos and video START takes a photo. UP starts a video and UP again stops it, with the recording time counting on your watch.
Confirmed on your wrist Your watch buzzes only once the photo is actually saved, and keeps count, so you know the shot happened without walking back to check.
Built-in camera too Prefer not to give the phone app accessibility access? Use CamRemote's own camera instead - photos and videos go straight to your gallery.
Knows when the phone isn't ready If the phone app is closed or the watch is out of range, the watch says so instead of pretending a photo was taken.
Made for every screen Crisp on round AMOLED and MIP screens alike, with marks beside the START and UP buttons and the Instinct's sub-display showing your photo count.

Privacy

Your photos stay on your phone

The watch and the phone talk only through Garmin Connect. Photos and videos are saved to your phone's gallery and never leave it. There are no accounts and no analytics.

On Android, the optional accessibility access is used for one thing: finding and pressing the shutter and record buttons in your phone's camera app when your watch asks. It reads no other app and collects nothing.

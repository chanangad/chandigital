How it works

Padel scoring, Golden Point and all

Padel shares tennis's 15-30-40 scoring, and Padel Go handles the whole ladder — deuce, advantage, games and sets, with a 7-point tiebreak at six games all. Switch on the Golden Point and a single sudden-death point at deuce decides the game instead, exactly as most clubs and tournaments play it.

Because padel is almost always doubles, the app is built around it — tracking the serving player, the box for the underhand serve, and which side each pair is on, point after point. You keep your eyes on the glass and the volley, not the score. Every match saves to Garmin Connect as a recorded activity with average and max heart rate, calories, elapsed time and per-set scores.

How it works

Pickleball's serving rules, handled

Pickleball scoring trips up more players than the rallies do - only the serving side can score, games go to 11 (win by 2), and in doubles both partners serve before the side-out. Pickleball Go tracks all of it: the score, who is serving, first server versus second server, and when the serve passes to the other team.

The court diagram shows the correct service side - right when the serving team's score is even, left when it's odd - and singles or doubles rules are applied automatically as you tap. Every game is written to Garmin Connect as a recorded activity with average and max heart rate, calories, elapsed time and per-game scores.

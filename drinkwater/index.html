Availability

Free while in beta

Drink Water! is in an open beta, and every feature is unlocked for everyone. There is nothing to buy and nothing to enter - install it from Connect IQ and it works.

The beta is here to find the last rough edges across the whole Garmin range. If something misbehaves, or a size or setting you need is missing, tell me and it usually ships the same week.

Features

Built to be faster than reaching for your phone

Two taps to log a glass One press brings up your sizes, the next logs it. The list opens on whatever you drank last, so your usual glass is always the quickest.
Buttons or touch Everything works with the physical buttons, and everything works by tapping and swiping. Neither is an afterthought.
Your sizes, your goal Up to five quick-add amounts and any daily target you like, set on the watch or from Garmin Connect Mobile.
Undo right there An undo button sits next to your drink sizes and takes back the last entry instantly, so a mistap costs nothing.
Reminders that are not nagging It works out how much you should have drunk by now and only nudges when you are genuinely behind. Set your waking hours and it stays quiet at night.
Goals that follow training Turn on Auto Goal and your target rises on days you record an activity, because a long run needs more than a rest day.
Seven and thirty day history Page down for bar charts of the past week and month against your goal, with your average and how many days you hit it.
Glance and complication Today's total, goal and percentage in the widget carousel, and published as a complication any supporting watch face can show.
Millilitres or fluid ounces Your choice of units, and it resets itself at midnight with yesterday filed into history automatically.

Good to know

One Garmin limitation

Garmin does not let third-party apps write to the built-in hydration log, so your intake is tracked inside this widget and will not appear on Garmin Connect's hydration card. Everything stays on your watch. That is a platform limit rather than a choice, and it applies to every third-party hydration app on Connect IQ.

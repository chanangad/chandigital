Refund Policy

When and how we issue refunds for Chan Digital app purchases.

Last updated: July 23, 2026

1. 24-hour refund window

We offer refunds within 24 hours of purchase for Badminton Go, Pickleball Go, Tennis Go, Padel Go, and any other Chan Digital app, but only in the following cases:

  • The app or watch face does not work properly on your device.
  • You are unable to unlock or activate your purchase after following the restore steps.
  • You've contacted support and the issue could not be resolved within the 24-hour window.

Refund requests made outside these cases, or after 24 hours have passed, are handled at our discretion.

2. What isn't covered

Change-of-mind purchases, or dissatisfaction with features that were clearly described on the product page before purchase, are not eligible for a refund. We encourage you to review the app's features and requirements on the product page before purchasing to confirm it works as expected on your device.

3. How to request a refund

Email with your claim code, the app name, and a description of the issue. We'll respond and, if eligible, process your refund to the original payment method within a few business days.

4. Payment processor timelines

Refunds are issued through Razorpay (India) or Dodo Payments (international), the same processor used for your original purchase. Depending on your bank, it can take 5–10 business days for a refund to appear on your statement after we process it.

Privacy Policy

How Chan Digital handles data for the Pill Time! app and website.

Last updated: September 30, 2026

In short: your medicine list lives on your watch and in your phone's browser. There are no accounts. When you send the list to your watch it passes through our server encrypted, so we can't read it, and it is deleted within 10 minutes. The one exception is scanning a prescription, which is optional and explained in section 4.

1. Who we are

Chan Digital ("we", "us", "our") operates the Pill Time! watch widget for Garmin and the website at chandigital.in/pilltime/. Contact us at .

2. Data kept on your watch

Pill Time! stores these on the watch, in Garmin's app storage:

  • Your medicines - names, doses, schedules, course dates, supply counts and settings such as time-critical or paused.
  • Your dose log - when each dose was taken, skipped or missed, the last 31 days of history, and your streak.
  • Settings - reminder and quiet-hour preferences.
  • License state - a claim code, the license status and when the free trial started.

None of this is sent to us. If you use the watch's own sleep times for quiet hours, the app reads them on the watch; they are not sent anywhere.

3. What the watch sends

  • License checks - the claim code, to chandigital.in, to see whether the watch is unlocked.
  • Collecting your list - while the "Add or update meds" screen is open, the watch asks our server for an encrypted parcel stored under an id derived from the code it shows, then asks the server to delete it.
  • Allowing scans - when that screen opens, the watch tells our server that a scan may be made for the next 15 minutes, sending the same id and its claim code.

These requests go through the Garmin Connect app on your phone. No medicine names, doses or dose history are ever sent by the watch.

4. The set-up page and prescription scanning

Your list. The set-up page at chandigital.in/pilltime/add keeps your list in your phone's browser storage. It is not sent to us unless you press Send. When you do, it is encrypted in the browser (AES) with a key derived from the code on your watch. Our server stores only the encrypted parcel, cannot decrypt it, and deletes it when your watch collects it or after 10 minutes, whichever is first.

Scanning (optional). If you choose to scan a prescription, label or medicine box, the photo is reduced in size on your phone, which also removes its location data, and is then sent unencrypted to our server and on to Anthropic, whose Claude AI model reads it and returns the medicine details to the page. We do not store or log the photo or what was read from it. Anthropic processes it under its commercial terms, which do not allow using it to train its models; see Anthropic's privacy policy. Nothing read from a photo is added to your list until you check and save it.

To limit misuse of scanning we keep, per scan session, the id described in section 3, the claim code and the number of scans, for up to 30 days, and a daily total of scans. To limit misuse of the relay and scanning we also keep a count of requests per IP address for one hour.

5. Data collected when you buy

When you purchase a license at chandigital.in/pilltime/, we collect:

  • Name and email address - to send your license confirmation and invoice, and to handle refund requests.
  • Claim code - the code from your watch that the license is tied to.
  • State (India only) - required for the GST invoice.
  • Payment information - processed directly by Razorpay (India) or Dodo Payments / PayPal (international). We do not see or store card numbers or payment credentials.

6. How we use your data

  • To activate your license, send your invoice and help with support requests.
  • To process refunds.
  • To keep the relay and scanning working and to limit their misuse.

We do not sell, rent or share your personal data for marketing. We never receive your medicine list in a form we can read, apart from a photo you choose to scan.

7. Where data is stored

License, purchase and scan-session records are stored in a Cloudflare database; the encrypted relay parcels and request counts in Cloudflare's key-value store. Payment processors keep their own records under their own privacy policies.

8. Cookies and analytics

chandigital.in does not use analytics cookies or tracking scripts, so no cookie banner is shown. The pages load fonts from Google Fonts. The set-up page stores your list, and whether you have agreed to the scanning notice, in your browser; that data stays on your device.

9. Data retention

Purchase records (name, email, claim code) are kept for up to 3 years for support, refunds and legal compliance. Scan-session records are kept for up to 30 days, relay parcels for at most 10 minutes, and per-IP request counts for one hour. You can ask for deletion at any time by emailing .

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data. Email with "Privacy Request" in the subject line.

11. Not a medical device

Pill Time! is a reminder and logging aid. It does not provide medical advice or check doses or interactions, and a reminder can be late or missed. Always follow your prescriber's or pharmacist's instructions.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version. Continued use of the app or website after a change means you accept the updated policy.

About the app

Let's set up your medicines

Takes about a minute. Your watch will remind you at every dose, and you log it with one press.

1Connect your watch

On the watch, open Pill Time!, press MENU and choose Add or update meds. Type the code it shows. Keep that screen open while you're here; the code lasts 10 minutes.

2Your medicines

✍️Add manually

Pick when you take it, then type the name.

📷Scan a prescription or label

Enter your watch's code above to scan. We read the photo and fill in the details, and you check each one before it's added.

... This can take up to half a minute.

When

Nothing reminds you. Log each dose on the watch with one press; it shows when the next one is fine and how many you have had today. 0 = no limit.

Every other day: take 1, pause 1. Every 3 days: take 1, pause 2. A 21/7 pack: take 21, pause 7. Counted from the start date.

The next reminder counts from when you actually took the last dose. The first time below is the first dose. 0 = no daily limit.

Course

Leave the number of days empty for a medicine you take long term. Reminders stop on their own when a course ends.

Supply

3Send to your watch

Sends your whole list to the watch showing the code. Send again after any change.

4Keep a backup

Your list lives only on this phone. Phones sometimes clear browser data, so a backup file lets you restore it here or on another phone.

Private by design. Your list is encrypted on this phone with a key from the watch's code. Our server only ever holds the scrambled result, for 10 minutes at most, and the watch deletes it as soon as it has it.
Scanning is the one exception. A photo you scan is sent, unencrypted, to our server and to an AI service (Anthropic's Claude) to read it. It is not stored or used for training, and it is discarded once read. Nothing is added until you check and save it. You can always add medicines manually instead. Privacy policy.
Not a medical device. Pill Time! is a reminder aid. It does not give medical advice, and a reminder can be late or missed (for example if the watch is off or in Do Not Disturb). Always follow your prescriber's instructions.

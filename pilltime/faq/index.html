FAQ

Pill Time!

Setting up your medicines, how reminders work, the trial and unlock, and getting support.

How do I set up my medicines?

Open Pill Time! on your watch. The first time, it shows a 12-character code; later you'll find it under MENU, Add or update meds. On your phone, open chandigital.in/pilltime/add, type the watch's code, add each medicine and when you take it, and press Send to watch. The watch picks the list up within a few seconds and tells you when your first reminder is.

Can I scan my prescription instead of typing?

Yes. On the set-up page, after entering the watch's code, choose Scan a prescription or label and take a photo of the prescription, pharmacy label or box. Pill Time! reads it and shows each medicine as a draft for you to check. Nothing is added until you save it, and anything it couldn't read clearly is flagged. Handwriting is the hardest case, so always compare with the label.

What happens when a dose is due?

Your watch buzzes with a notification that shows the medicine and has three buttons: Taken, Snooze and Skip. If you don't answer, it reminds you again (every 10 minutes by default, up to 3 more times), then waits. If the dose still isn't answered by the cutoff (2 hours by default), it's marked missed and you get one last notice with a Took it late button. You can change all of these in the app's Reminders menu or in Garmin Connect.

How do I log a dose without the notification?

Open the widget. When something is due, the screen shows it and a single press of START marks everything due as taken. Pressed by mistake? BACK undoes it for a few seconds. To handle medicines one at a time, snooze or skip, hold the screen or press MENU.

What kinds of schedule can I set?

Every day at set times; on chosen days of the week; every few days or on/off cycles such as a 21-day pack with a 7-day break; every few hours counted from when you actually took the last dose (with an optional daily maximum); and as needed. A course can have a length in days, after which reminders stop by themselves.

How do as-needed medicines work?

They never remind you. Open My meds, pick the medicine and choose Log a dose now. The watch then shows when the next dose is fine ("OK after 14:30") and how many you've had today against your limit. If you log one earlier or over the limit, it asks you to confirm, but it never stops you: Pill Time! records, it doesn't advise.

Will it wake me up at night?

Not unless you want it to. Set quiet hours in the Reminders menu, either custom times or your watch's own sleep schedule. Reminders that fall inside them wait until the end. A medicine marked time-critical on the set-up page always reminds you on time.

Does it warn me before I run out?

If you turn on supply counting for a medicine, it counts down as you take doses and warns you once a day when you're a few days from running out. When you refill, open the medicine under My meds and choose Refilled. A course that your supply already covers is never flagged.

What if I travel to another time zone?

Medicines set for clock times follow local time, so an 08:00 dose is at 08:00 wherever you are. Medicines set as every few hours keep their true spacing. If a medicine must stay exactly spaced across time zones, set it as every few hours rather than at clock times.

Why was a reminder late, or didn't arrive?

Garmin lets an app wake up at most once every 5 minutes, so reminders can be up to a few minutes late. They can also be held by Do Not Disturb or sleep mode on the watch, or missed if the watch was off. If the home screen says Background blocked, the watch isn't letting Pill Time! run in the background; reinstalling the app and allowing its permissions fixes that. Pill Time! is a reminder aid, so please don't rely on it alone for a medicine where a missed dose would be dangerous.

Where is my data kept?

On your watch and in your phone's browser. There are no accounts. When you send your list, it's encrypted on your phone with a key only your watch can work out, and our server deletes the scrambled parcel as soon as the watch collects it, or after 10 minutes. The page keeps your list only in this browser, so use Save backup file to keep a copy. Scanning is the one exception: see the privacy policy.

How does the free trial and unlock work?

Every install has a 24-hour free trial with everything switched on. When it ends, reminders stop until you unlock; your medicines, history and streak stay on the watch. To unlock, find the claim code on the watch (MENU, Unlock), enter it on chandigital.in/pilltime and pay once. Open the app with your phone nearby and it unlocks itself within a few seconds, with reminders back on from the next dose. There is no subscription.

I got a new watch. Can I move my license?

No. Each license belongs to the watch it was bought for, because the claim code comes from that watch. Reinstalling on the same watch keeps the same code and it unlocks itself again. To move your medicines, restore your backup on the set-up page and send it to the new watch. If something doesn't unlock as expected, with your claim code and purchase email.

Which watches are supported?

63 Garmin watches from API 5.1, including fenix 7, 8 and 9, epix Pro, Enduro 3, Forerunner 165 to 970, Venu 3, 4 and X1, vivoactive 5 and 6, Instinct 3 AMOLED, D2, Descent, Approach and MARQ. The Venu 2 family isn't supported, because it can't show notifications with Taken and Snooze buttons.

Is Pill Time! a medical device?

No. It is a reminder and logging aid. It doesn't give medical advice, doesn't check doses or interactions, and a reminder can be late or missed. Always follow your prescriber's or pharmacist's instructions.

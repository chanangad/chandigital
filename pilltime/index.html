Setting up

Type your meds on your phone, get reminded on your wrist

  1. Open Pill Time! on your watch On first launch it shows a short code. Later, it's under MENU, Add or update meds.
  2. Add your meds on your phone Open the set-up page, type in each medicine and when you take it, or scan the prescription or the pharmacy label.
  3. Send it to the watch Enter the watch's code and press Send. The watch says when the first reminder is, and you're done.

Features

Built so a dose never slips by quietly

Reminders that don't give up If you don't answer, it reminds you again, every 10 minutes by default, then tells you straight when a dose was missed. You choose how often and how many times.
Answer from the notification Every reminder has Taken, Snooze and Skip. Pick one and you're done - no app to open, no menu to find.
One press to log Open the widget when something's due and press START: everything due is marked taken. BACK undoes it for a few seconds, in case you pressed by mistake.
Did I take it? The glance answers without opening anything: "Taken 08:02", "Due now" or "Next 14:00, in 1 h 20 m".
Every kind of schedule Every day, certain weekdays, every few days, 21-on-7-off packs, or every few hours counted from when you actually took the last one.
As-needed meds For painkillers and the like: log a dose with one press, and the watch shows when the next one is fine and how many you've had today.
Courses that end themselves Set a seven-day course and it counts down "Day 3 of 7", then stops and tells you it's finished.
Refill warnings Tell it how many you have and it counts them down, warning you a few days before you run out. One press on the watch when you refill.
Quiet at night Quiet hours follow your watch's sleep schedule or times you set. Medicines marked time-critical still remind you on time.
Streaks and a weekly summary See how many days in a row you've taken everything, and get a short summary every Sunday evening. History for 7 and 30 days, with late doses marked.
On your watch face Pill Time! publishes a complication any supporting watch face can show: your next dose time, "!" when one is due, and a tick once today is done.

Privacy

Your medicines stay yours

There are no accounts. Your list lives in your phone's browser and on your watch. When you send it, it is encrypted on the phone with a key derived from the code your watch shows, and only your watch can work that key out. Our server passes the encrypted parcel along without being able to read it, and deletes it once the watch collects it or after 10 minutes.

Scanning a prescription is the one exception, and it's optional: the photo is sent to our server and to an AI service to read it, then discarded. It is never stored. Privacy policy.

Pill Time! is a reminder aid, not a medical device, and it does not give medical advice. A reminder can be late or missed, for example if your watch is off or in Do Not Disturb. Always follow your prescriber's instructions.

FAQ

Squash Go

Common questions about installation, scoring, purchasing, and getting support on Garmin watches.

Which Garmin devices does Squash Go support?

Squash Go supports 74 Garmin wearables with a Connect IQ app display, including Fenix 7/8 series, Forerunner 255/265/955/965, Venu 2/3, Epix, Instinct 2, Vivoactive 4/5, and more. Check the full compatibility list on the Connect IQ listing.

How does scoring work?

During a game, swipe down (or press DOWN) when you win the rally, and swipe up (or press UP) when your opponent wins it. The score updates instantly, the server and the service box move by themselves, and Game Ball and Match Ball banners appear when a player is one rally away.

Which scoring systems does Squash Go support?

Two, chosen under Scoring rules before the match:

  • PAR (default) - point-a-rally: every rally scores and the winner serves next. Games to 11, win by two. Points to Win takes any target from 5 to 50, and Win by 2 can be turned off.
  • English - hand-in/hand-out to 9: only the server scores, and a receiver who wins the rally takes the serve. At 8-all, choose Set 1 (to 9) or Set 2 (to 10).

Either way, matches are best of 3 or best of 5 games. Squash Go is built to the World Squash singles rules.

How does the service box tracking work?

The bottom half of the screen is the court, seen from behind the back wall. The highlighted half is the side you are standing on, and a ball marks the box the serve comes from. The server switches boxes after every rally they win, and a new server starts in the right box. A new server may choose either box, so a single tap during play moves the serve to the other box.

What if I score the wrong rally?

Press the BACK button during a game to undo the last rally. Squash Go keeps a full history for the current game, so you can step back as many times as you need.

Does it support doubles?

No. Squash Go is a singles scorekeeper, built to the World Squash singles rules.

What happens between games?

When a game ends, the screen counts down the 90-second interval so you know when to get back on court. Choose Next Game to carry on in the same activity, or Warm-Up first. Once the match is decided, Save & Exit saves it.

How do I unlock the app?

Squash Go has a 24-hour free trial. During the trial the claim code is under Unlock App in the pre-game menu (and as Watch ID under About), and when it ends the watch shows a lock screen with the code. It looks like SQ- followed by 12 digits, shown in groups of four. Visit chandigital.in/squash, enter the claim code and complete payment. Then just open the app on your watch with your phone nearby - it notices the payment and unlocks itself within a few seconds. No menu steps needed.

Does one purchase unlock more than one watch?

No - one purchase unlocks one watch, for life. The claim code you pay against is generated by the watch itself, so the license belongs to that watch rather than to your email address. It survives uninstalling and reinstalling the app on the same watch. A second watch shows a different claim code and needs its own license.

I got a new watch. Can I transfer my license?

No - each license belongs to the watch it was bought on. Your claim code is generated from that watch, so reinstalling Squash Go on the same watch keeps the same code, and the app unlocks itself again the next time you open it with your phone nearby. A different watch has its own claim code and needs its own unlock. If you reinstalled on the same watch and it didn't unlock, email contact@chandigital.in with your claim code and purchase email and we'll sort it out.

My watch hasn't unlocked after payment.

Make sure your watch is connected to your phone and has an active internet connection via Bluetooth, then reopen the app - it checks for the payment on open, and keeps checking for a few minutes while the unlock screen is open. Refresh License on the unlock screen forces a check immediately. If it still shows as locked, with the claim code shown on the watch.

Does Squash Go record a Garmin activity?

Yes. When you end a match and choose Save & Exit, Squash Go saves it to Garmin Connect as a squash activity with heart rate (if your device supports it), duration and calories. The Stats tab shows Winner, Games Won/Lost, Total Points, Your Points, Opponent Points, Game Scores and Games Played, and a chart shows the point difference through the match.

Does it use GPS?

No. Squash is played indoors, so Squash Go never turns GPS on. That saves battery, and there is no waiting for a GPS fix before you can start.

What does the bottom stat show, and can I change it?

The edge of the game screen shows either the current time of day or the elapsed match timer. Go to Settings → Bottom Stat from the pre-game menu to switch between Clock and Timer. The same Settings menu holds the court theme (Glass Court, Maple or Club Green) and haptics.

Can I see my past matches?

Yes. Open the app and choose Past Games in the pre-game menu to see your last 20 matches with date, result, game-by-game scores and duration. Select any match for the full summary. You can also clear history from that list.
